№ 02 — The difference

One mill,
every thread

Most bedding brands design in one city, weave in another, and never meet the loom. We are the loom. Every bolt of cloth is woven, dyed, sewn and folded at Rua das Tecelagens 14 — which is why we can promise what we promise.
