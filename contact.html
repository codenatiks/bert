The house FAQ

Order, sleep, decide over a full season. If it isn't right, write to us any time within 100 nights — we arrange collection and refund in full. Washed, lived-in cloth is fine; that is the entire point of the trial.

Hot sleeper or crisp-hotel lover: percale. You like drape and a faint sheen: sateen. You want texture that gets better with age: linen. Cold winters and cold ears: flannel. If you'd like, email us the light in your room and we'll advise.

Everything in stock leaves Porto within 48 hours, wrapped in paper and string. Europe arrives in 2–4 working days; everywhere else in 4–8. Orders over $150 ship free.

30°C, gentle cycle, mild detergent, no fabric softener (it coats the fibre). Line-dry in the shade if you can. Linen wants to be wrinkled; percale wants a light iron while damp; flannel wants nothing but to be left alone.
